What to include

  • Your Support code from Data safety center > Privacy.
  • Your device, operating system, and RonikCloud app version.
  • What you expected, what happened, and steps that reproduce the issue.
  • The approximate time of the problem and any non-sensitive error message.

Please do not send passwords, encryption passphrases, authentication codes, private keys, recovery material, or payment-card numbers. Share your account email only when support must verify ownership for billing, account access, or a privacy request.

Response guidance

Support requests are reviewed on Czech business days. We aim to acknowledge account, billing, storage, and privacy requests within two business days. Security reports and locked-account requests are prioritized. Complex data-recovery investigations may take longer, and we will keep you updated by email.

Account closure, payment details, and refunds

Canceling renewal, requesting account closure, and requesting a refund are separate actions. The authenticated in-app closure request does not immediately sign you out, end access, cancel billing, or delete data. Cancel renewal separately if desired. Completed closure does not automatically issue a refund. RonikCloud never needs your full card number or card security code.

Review the dedicated account-deletion page before closing an account, especially if the subscription is billed through Apple.
