Choose Business when files belong to a company and people need different access. Keep a Personal workspace for the files you manage yourself.

Personal or Business?

Personal starts with 1 GB free and supports protected sharing. Business adds a company workspace, member administration, groups, and permissions for shared files.

Business has a USD 6 minimum total monthly service charge before tax. Above the minimum, the subtotal is approved attributable service cost divided by 0.50. Review and accept the disclosed cost schedule, storage capacity and automatic payment authorization before activation. Company-sponsored storage does not require a separate Personal subscription.
